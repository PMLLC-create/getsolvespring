Thirty dollars an hour is a common benchmark for skilled trades, healthcare support roles, and many office jobs. Here's what it adds up to over a year, a month, and a paycheck.

## The math

<p class="formula">$30 × 40 hours × 52 weeks = $62,400</p>

A full-time year is 2,080 hours. Every extra dollar per hour adds $2,080 a year, so $30 an hour earns $10,400 more per year than $25 an hour.

## $30 an hour by pay period

| Pay period | Gross pay at $30/hour |
|---|---:|
| Per year | $62,400 |
| Per month | $5,200 |
| Twice a month (24 paychecks) | $2,600 |
| Every two weeks (26 paychecks) | $2,400 |
| Per week | $1,200 |
| Per 8-hour day | $240 |

$62,400 divides evenly by 12, so the monthly average is exactly $5,200. Your actual deposits depend on how often you're paid.

## Part-time, full-time, and overtime

Overtime rows below use time and a half ($45 an hour) for hours over 40.

| Hours per week | Weekly pay | Yearly pay |
|---|---:|---:|
| 20 | $600 | $31,200 |
| 30 | $900 | $46,800 |
| 35 | $1,050 | $54,600 |
| 40 | $1,200 | $62,400 |
| 45 (5 hours overtime) | $1,425 | $74,100 |
| 50 (10 hours overtime) | $1,650 | $85,800 |

Five hours of overtime a week adds $11,700 a year. That's real money, but it's also 260 extra hours. Worth knowing when you compare an hourly job with overtime against a salaried one.

## $30 an hour after taxes

A simplified 2026 federal estimate for a **single filer**, standard deduction, no pre-tax deductions, no credits:

| Item | Per year |
|---|---:|
| Gross pay | $62,400.00 |
| Federal income tax | −$5,308.00 |
| Social Security (6.2%) | −$3,868.80 |
| Medicare (1.45%) | −$904.80 |
| **Take-home before state tax** | **$52,318.40** |

That's about **$4,360 a month** or **$2,012.25 every two weeks** before any state or local income tax.

The federal income tax works like this: $62,400 minus the $16,100 standard deduction leaves $46,300 of taxable income. The first $12,400 is taxed at 10% ($1,240) and the next $33,900 at 12% ($4,068). Even though your top bracket is 12%, your federal income tax is only about 8.5% of gross pay.

## Is $30 an hour a good wage?

It depends on where you live and what your job typically pays. The same $62,400 stretches much further in a low-cost area than in a big coastal city. Two useful ways to judge it:

1. **Compare it to your local cost of living.** Build a monthly budget from your expected take-home pay and see what's left after housing.
2. **Compare it to your occupation.** The Bureau of Labor Statistics publishes wage data by occupation and metro area in its Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics program. Look up your job title and area.

## Common questions

### How much is $30 an hour biweekly?

$2,400 before taxes, assuming 80 hours in the two-week period.

### What salary is equal to $30 an hour?

$62,400 a year at 40 hours a week. If a salaried job expects 45 hours a week, you'd need about $70,200 to match $30 an hour.

### How much is $30 an hour for 35 hours a week?

$1,050 a week and $54,600 a year before taxes.
